11 · System

Formats & testing loop.

Formats
~50/50 video vs static. Template barely changes: native Slack exchange, short benefit line, "VIKTOR.COM" card. Brand videos 0:08–0:55, hook-first. Creator videos 0:19–3:00, median 1:20. Product-as-creative: user @mentions Viktor, Viktor replies with numbers, a PDF or a link.
Testing loop
~90 new concepts a week at September peak; 1 in 4 got a single ad. Winners relaunched month after month: 103 concepts ran in 3+ months. Most new ads off within a day (61–75% in Jul–Sep). Advantage+ placements, no placement-specific creative.
