Formats & testing system

Formats
Mix~50/50 video vs static. Template barely changes: native Slack exchange, short benefit line, "VIKTOR.COM" card.
LengthBrand videos 0:08–0:55, hook-first ("POV: you counted your open tabs…"). Creator videos 0:19–3:00, median 1:20.
Product as creativeUser @mentions Viktor, Viktor replies with numbers, a PDF or a link.
Copy formulaQuantified pain → "AI employee in Slack or Teams" → proof number (45,000+ → 60,000+ teams) → viktor.com.
Testing loop
Volume~90 new concepts a week at the September peak; 1 in 4 got a single ad.
Winner relaunches103 concepts ran in 3+ months, relaunched month after month.
Short flightsMost new ads off within a day (61–75% in Jul – Sep).
PlacementsAdvantage+ placements (3–4 Meta platforms per card). No placement-specific creative found.
